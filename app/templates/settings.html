Changing this moves the singles you already have and updates your playlists. YTMLS also puts a small artist.nfo file in those artist folders, because Jellyfin would otherwise show an artist you only have singles from as one album named after the artist.

YouTube's streams are already compressed, so 320 kbps is the setting that loses the least extra quality. Spreading a large first sync over several runs is gentler on your account.

Notifications

You'll get a browser notification when something new shows up in your library (so you can decide whether to sync it), after every sync with a short summary, and when a sync fails.
