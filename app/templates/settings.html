How to export cookies that last. The usual reason cookies stop working after a day is that the browser you exported from keeps using the same session. Google then gives the browser new cookie values and the exported copy stops working. Exporting from a private window that you then close leaves YTMLS as the only user of that session.

  1. Open a new private/incognito window. Close any other private windows first.
  2. Sign in at music.youtube.com in that window.
  3. In the same tab, go to https://music.youtube.com/robots.txt. This plain page doesn't run YouTube's scripts, so the cookies don't change while you export.
  4. Export cookies for music.youtube.com in Netscape cookies.txt format, for example with the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" extension. You may need to allow the extension in private windows.
  5. Close the private window right away, without signing out. Signing out would end the session YTMLS is about to use.
  6. Upload the file or paste its contents below. A raw Cookie: header copied from your browser's network tab also works, but a cookies.txt file lasts longer.

Your normal browser windows stay signed in as usual. Each sign-in is its own session, so YouTube in your normal browser doesn't affect this one.

Sync

{{ sync_error }}

{% endif %}

Songs are saved in the best quality YouTube Music offers your account, exactly as it sends them, with no re-encoding. Without Premium that's Opus at about 160 kbps (.opus files). Jellyfin plays these and converts them on the fly for any player that can't.

Changing this moves the singles you already have and updates your playlists. YTMLS also puts a small artist.nfo file in those artist folders, because Jellyfin would otherwise show an artist you only have singles from as one album named after the artist.

Spreading a large first sync over several runs is gentler on your account.

Notifications

You'll get a browser notification when something new shows up in your library (so you can decide whether to sync it), after every sync with a short summary, and when a sync fails.
